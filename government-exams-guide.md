# Government Exams — Detailed Exam-by-Exam Guide

*Last updated: 22 October 2026*

Every exam below follows the same layout: at-a-glance facts, posts, eligibility with age by category, fee, selection steps, exam pattern, syllabus, physical and medical standards, salary, job profile and promotion, and preparation tips. Confirm dates and vacancies from the official notification before applying.

**How to read this guide.** Each time-sensitive fact is linked to its source where one was checked. Dates are labelled *official* (announced in a notice or official site), *tentative* (announced as provisional) or *typical* (based on past cycles). Salaries are *estimates*. Age limits, exam patterns, fees and physical standards follow the latest notices seen as of October 2026 and change between cycles and posts. Use this guide to find the right exam, then confirm eligibility in that exam's official notification. A summary table cannot confirm that a candidate is eligible.

**Tags used in this guide:** 10th Pass · No Written Exam · High Salary · High Vacancy · Women Reservation · No Physical Test. A dash (—) means none of the six apply. Women Reservation means a reserved share or separate vacancies for women, not just that women may apply. In MP, 35% of posts are reserved for women in MPPSC and ESB recruitment, except the Forest Department ([source](https://news.careers360.com/mp-cabinet-raises-quota-for-women-in-state-government-jobs-35-percent/amp)).

| Tag | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| 10th Pass | 10th is the minimum qualification |
| No Written Exam | Selection by SSB, shortlisting, merit list or interview only |
| High Salary | Officer-level pay (roughly Level 7+ or bank officer scale) |
| High Vacancy | Large recruitment, usually thousands of posts |
| Women Reservation | A reserved share or separate vacancies for women |
| No Physical Test | No running or physical efficiency test |

## Part 1 — SSC (Staff Selection Commission)

Fees and fee exemptions vary by exam, so check each notice. In recent notices most SSC exams charge ₹100, with women, SC, ST, PwBD and ex-servicemen exempt. Apply only on [ssc.gov.in](https://ssc.gov.in) after One-Time Registration (OTR). Aadhaar authentication is optional for CGL 2026, and other identity documents are accepted ([source](https://www.mahendras.org/blogs/ssc-cgl-2026-exam-date-eligibility-and-age-limit-explained-complete-guide)). Aadhaar checks do apply to the scribe rules ([source](https://www.pw.live/news/ssc-cgl-2026-major-update-exam-pattern-vacancies)).

### 1.1 SSC CGL (Combined Graduate Level)

SSC CGL is the biggest graduate-level central exam, with 12,256 vacancies in the 2026 cycle and pay up to Level 8.

**At a glance**

| Item | Detail |

| Interview | No |
| Tags | High Salary (AAO, ASO, Inspector posts), High Vacancy, No Physical Test (most posts) |

**Posts and departments**

| Post | Department | Pay Level |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Assistant Audit Officer (Central and State Cadre) | C&AG — Indian Audit & Accounts Department (Group B Gazetted) | Level 8 |
| Assistant Accounts Officer | C&AG / CGA (Group B Gazetted) | Level 8 |
| Assistant Section Officer | Central Secretariat, MEA, Railways, IB, AFHQ | Level 7 |
| Assistant Enforcement Officer | Enforcement Directorate | Level 7 |
| Inspector (Central Excise, GST, Preventive Officer, Examiner) | CBIC | Level 7 |
| Inspector of Income Tax | CBDT | Level 7 |
| Sub-Inspector | CBI, NIA | Level 7 / 6 |
| Divisional Accountant | C&AG | Level 6 |
| Junior Statistical Officer / Statistical Investigator | MoSPI, RGI | Level 6 |
| Auditor, Accountant, Junior Accountant | C&AG, CGDA, CGA | Level 5 |

AAO and Assistant Accounts Officer posts returned in CGL 2026 with a Central and State Cadre structure at Level 8 ([source](https://www.sscdrishti.com/blog/ssc-cgl-aao-2026-key-dates-level-8-salary-new-exam-pattern), [source](https://www.businesstoday.in/amp/education/exams/story/new-changes-introduced-in-ssc-cgl-2026-recruitment-heres-what-aspirants-should-know-533333-2026-05-28)). The final post list and vacancies are in the official notice on ssc.gov.in.

**Eligibility**

- **Nationality:** Indian citizen (or Nepal/Bhutan subjects and certain migrants as per rules)
- **Qualification:** Bachelor's degree in any subject. JSO needs 60% in Maths at 12th or Statistics in graduation. Final-year students can apply if they finish before the cut-off date
- **Age:** Post-wise — 18–27, 20–30 or 18–32 (cut-off date 1 August of the exam year)

| Category | Upper age (for an 18–30 post) |
| --- | --- |
| UR / EWS | 30 |
| OBC (NCL) | 33 |
| SC / ST | 35 |
| PwBD (UR / OBC / SC-ST) | 40 / 43 / 45 |
| Ex-Servicemen | 3 years after deducting service |

**Selection process**

1. Tier 1 — qualifying screening (marks not counted in final merit)
2. Tier 2 — merit exam (Paper I for all; Paper II for JSO; Paper III for AAO and Assistant Accounts Officer)
3. Skill tests inside Tier 2 — Computer Knowledge module and Data Entry Speed Test (DEST)
4. Physical / medical test for CBI SI, Inspector (CBIC, Narcotics) posts
5. Document verification and post allotment by merit and preference

**Exam pattern — Tier 1 (60 minutes)**

| Section | Questions | Marks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| General Intelligence & Reasoning | 25 | 50 |
| General Awareness | 25 | 50 |
| Quantitative Aptitude | 25 | 50 |
| English Comprehension | 25 | 50 |
| Total | 100 | 200 |

Negative marking: 0.50 per wrong answer. The 2026 notice introduced sectional timing in Tier 1 ([source](https://www.taiyarho.in/exams/ssc-cgl/)).

**Exam pattern — Tier 2 Paper I**

| Session / Section | Module | Questions | Marks | Time |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Section I | Mathematical Abilities + Reasoning | 30 + 30 | 180 | 1 hour |
| Section II | English + General Awareness | 45 + 25 | 210 | 1 hour |
| Section III | Computer Knowledge (qualifying) | 20 | 60 | 15 min |
| Session II | Data Entry Speed Test (qualifying) | 2,000 key depressions | — | 15 min |

Negative marking in Tier 2 differs by paper and section, so check the 2026 notice for the exact values.

**Post-specific papers in Tier 2**

| Paper | Who takes it | Subject | Questions / Marks |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Paper II | Shortlisted for JSO / Statistical Investigator | Statistics | 100 / 200, 2 hours |
| Paper III (new in 2026) | Shortlisted for Assistant Audit Officer / Assistant Accounts Officer | General Studies (Finance & Economics) | 100 / 200 |

For AAO posts, merit is based on Paper I plus Paper III ([source](https://www.sscdrishti.com/blog/ssc-cgl-aao-2026-key-dates-level-8-salary-new-exam-pattern), [source](https://www.pw.live/ssc/exams/new-changes-in-ssc-cgl-2026-recruitment)).

**Syllabus**

| Subject | Topics |
| --- | --- |
| Quantitative Aptitude | Number system, percentage, ratio, average, interest, profit & loss, time & work, speed & distance, mensuration, algebra, geometry, trigonometry, data interpretation, statistics |
| Reasoning | Analogy, classification, series, coding-decoding, blood relations, direction, syllogism, Venn diagram, matrix, paper folding, embedded figures, puzzles |
| English | Vocabulary, synonyms & antonyms, idioms, one-word substitution, spelling, error spotting, sentence improvement, active/passive, narration, cloze test, reading comprehension |
| General Awareness | History, geography, polity, economy, general science, static GK, current affairs, sports, awards, books |
| Computer | Computer basics, MS Office, internet, email, networking, cyber security |
| Statistics (JSO) | Collection & presentation of data, central tendency, dispersion, correlation & regression, probability, sampling, index numbers, time series |

**Paper III syllabus (AAO / Assistant Accounts Officer):** Finance (financial accounting, auditing basics, Finance Commission, budgeting) and Economics (micro and macro economics, growth and development, money and banking, Indian economy).

**Physical standards (only for some posts)**

| Post | Men | Women |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CBI Sub-Inspector | Height 165 cm, chest 76 + 5 cm, vision 6/6 | Height 150 cm |
| Inspector (CBIC, Narcotics) | 157.5 cm; walk 1,600 m in 15 min, cycle 8 km in 30 min | 152 cm; walk 1 km in 20 min, cycle 3 km in 25 min |

**Salary and perks**

- Level 4 (₹25,500 basic) to Level 8 (₹47,600 basic, AAO / Assistant Accounts Officer); estimated gross roughly ₹46,000 to ₹96,000 a month with 60% DA, HRA and transport allowance
- Perks: pension (NPS/UPS), medical (CGHS), LTC, government quarters, children education allowance

**Job profile and promotion**

- ASO handles files and policy notes in ministries; Inspectors do tax assessment, raids and customs duty; Auditors check government accounts
- Typical growth: ASO → Section Officer → Under Secretary; Inspector → Superintendent → Assistant Commissioner

**Preparation tips**

- Maths and English carry a large share of Tier 2 Paper I marks; AAO aspirants must also prepare Finance & Economics for Paper III; practise advanced maths (geometry, trigonometry, algebra) early
- Read one newspaper daily and revise static GK monthly
- Take full-length mocks with sectional timing; practise typing for DEST (about 30 wpm)

## Part 2 — Railway (RRB / RRC / RPF)

Railway fee for most RRB exams is ₹500, of which ₹400 is refunded after you appear in CBT 1. SC, ST, ex-servicemen, PwBD, women, transgender, minorities and economically backward classes pay ₹250, fully refunded after CBT 1. Apply on your chosen regional RRB website (one RRB only). Exams are in 15 languages. Recent RRB CBTs deduct 1/3 mark per wrong answer. Selection stages differ by exam and are listed under each one; check the CEN notice for your post. Railway employees get free travel passes and railway quarters.

## Part 6 — Defence (Army, Navy, Air Force, Coast Guard)

Defence jobs have two routes: Agniveer (4-year service for soldiers, sailors and airmen) and officer entries. Caste-based age relaxation generally does not apply. NDA and CDS are covered in Part 4. Army, Navy and Air Force run separate Agniveer recruitments with their own eligibility, exams and physical standards.

**Agniveer pay package (Army, Navy, Air Force)**

| Year | Monthly package (₹) | In hand (₹) | Corpus fund (₹) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 30,000 | 21,000 | 9,000 |
| 2 | 33,000 | 23,100 | 9,900 |
| 3 | 36,500 | 25,550 | 10,950 |
| 4 | 40,000 | 28,000 | 12,000 |

Under the Agnipath scheme, each Agniveer gets a Seva Nidhi of about ₹10.04 lakh (tax-free) at exit, and up to 25% of a batch can be selected for regular service. After-service benefits are set by each recruiting organisation. For example, MHA has announced reservation, age relaxation and PET exemption for ex-Agniveers in CAPF constable recruitment, and several states have announced police quotas. Check the specific notice for the percentage and conditions.

### 6.1 Army Agniveer (GD, Technical, Clerk / Store Keeper, Tradesman, Women Military Police)

The main route for 10th and 12th pass students to join the Army; the 2026 CEE was held in June 2026. Website: [joinindianarmy.nic.in](https://joinindianarmy.nic.in).

- **Syllabus:** GD — GK, General Science, Maths, Reasoning (10th level); Technical — Physics, Chemistry, Maths, GK; Clerk — GK, Science, Maths, Computer, English

### Common selection process

1. **PST (Physical Standard Test):** height and chest measurement
2. **PET (Physical Efficiency Test):** usually men 5 km in 24 min and women 1.6 km in 8.5 min for constable posts; men 1.6 km in 6.5 min for head constable posts
3. **Written test:** usually 100 questions, 100 marks, 2 hours — General Intelligence, General Knowledge, Elementary Maths, Hindi or English
4. **Trade / Skill test:** trade work for tradesmen, driving for drivers, typing (35 wpm English / 30 wpm Hindi) for ministerial posts, dictation for ASI Steno
5. **Document verification and Detailed Medical Exam**
